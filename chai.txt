Masala chai
Serves: 2

Ingredients:
- 1 cup water
- 1 cup milk
- 2 tsp tea leaves
- 2 tsp sugar
- 2 cardamom pods, crushed
- A small piece of ginger

Method:
1. Boil the water with the ginger and cardamom.
2. Add the tea leaves and boil for one minute.
3. Add the milk and sugar, boil again, then strain.
