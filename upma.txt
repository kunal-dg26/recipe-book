Upma
Serves: 2

Ingredients:
- 1 cup rava (semolina)
- 2 cups water
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
- A few curry leaves
- Salt to taste

Method:
1. Roast the rava until it smells nutty, then set it aside.
2. Fry the mustard seeds, curry leaves and onion.
3. Add the water and salt, boil, then stir in the rava.
