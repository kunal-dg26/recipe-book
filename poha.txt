Poha
Serves: 2

Ingredients:
- 2 cups thick poha
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 green chilli, chopped
- 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
- 1/4 tsp turmeric
- Salt to taste
- A squeeze of lemon

Method:
1. Rinse the poha and let it drain.
2. Fry the mustard seeds, onion and chilli.
3. Add turmeric, salt and the poha. Mix well.
